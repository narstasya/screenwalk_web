It's the beginning of September. And you have to know one thing about me: I have an irrational fear of autumn. Or maybe there are actually a couple of reasons why I feel this way... Shorter days, colder mornings... the beginning of school or uni. New people to meet and get used to for the upcoming semester. Harder assignments to tackle.

I can feel it. I can feel the change in the sun's angle, the birds singing differently, and some of them flying away to warmer places.

Maybe it's still warm and still sunny, but it's not the same. And this realisation hits me so hard that I want to collapse and cry because it hurts. It's the feeling of time slipping away right in front of you. Your unfinished plans to see a shooting star late at night, or to go swimming at least once a week. Things you can only do in the summer. All of it merges together and makes me so scared to live through autumn. I just can't.

But why? Is it that bad?
